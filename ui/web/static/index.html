    Why this step matters

    Most wrong results start with a misread Traffic Sheet. Check the counts and the colours here first.

    Colours drive the QA. Green rows must exist in Innovid, red rows must be gone, and yellow or grey rows are left alone.

    A column that is missing or barely filled is the first thing to check when a result looks wrong.

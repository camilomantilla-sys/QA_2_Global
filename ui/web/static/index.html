    How the columns were matched

    QA2 matches columns by their header name, never by position. “Filled” is the share of rows with a value.

    QA2 needsColumnHeader in the sheetFilled

    Why this step matters

    Most wrong results start with a misread Traffic Sheet. Check the counts and the colours here first.

    Colours drive the QA. Green rows must exist in Innovid, red rows must be gone, and yellow or grey rows are left alone.

    A column that is missing or barely filled is the first thing to check when a result looks wrong.

    Step 2

    Innovid exports

    Drop the files you downloaded from Innovid. QA2 says what each one is and how many of the placements in this request it found, before anything runs.

    Why this step matters

    The Placement-Creative View is the only file QA2 needs. It shows which creatives are assigned, their status and their click tags.

    The Placement View adds pixels and the URLs of 1x1 placements. Tag files and DV Pinnacle files turn on the tag and DoubleVerify checks.

    If a placement from the Traffic Sheet is missing here, check the export filter before running.

    Step 3 · optional

    Check against Innovid

    Three things are not in any export: each creative's flight dates inside the decision set, its rotation weight, and the verification partner. QA2 can read them from Innovid with your saved sign-in.

    Checking…
    Flight datesEach creative inside its decision set (INV-001)
    Rotation weightEven, 1x and percentages compared the same way (INV-002)
    Verification partnerDoubleVerify on DV Omni video (INV-003)

    When to use it

    Turn it on whenever the request changes dates or rotation. Without it, those checks come back as Not checked, never as a pass.

    The sign-in is saved on this computer only. QA2 never asks for your password here.
